How it works

1. Student joins

Open the Mathlab Zoom link and enter the main room hosted by the Mathlab Coordinator.

↓
2. Coordinator assigns a tutor

Tell the Coordinator your course and topic. They place you with one of the three undergraduate tutors (or the Founder/Instructor for advanced help).

Book / Join Zoom
↓
3. Tutor teaches in a breakout room

Work in a separate Zoom breakout room while your tutor writes on an iPad whiteboard — step by step, live.

Our approach

We help students move from memorizing steps to understanding ideas — so computations, proofs, and exams stop feeling mysterious.

Why Nigus Mathlab?

Rigor

We aim for mastery: definitions, examples, and the “why” behind each method — not just the final answer.

Results

Sessions are organized around your homework and exams, with practice that builds lasting technique.

Access

Live online support for students from high school through graduate coursework, at a clear and affordable rate.
