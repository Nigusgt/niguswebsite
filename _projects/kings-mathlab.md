**Kings Mathlab** is an online math tuition and homework-help center I founded and coordinated around 2021. Sessions ran over Zoom, with a main Math Lab host room and breakout rooms for one-to-one or small-group tutoring on a virtual whiteboard.

## Model

- Students join a shared Zoom Math Lab, then move into breakout rooms with a tutor
- Offerings spanned school-level, college-level, and advanced mathematics
- Focused on Northern California communities (Bay Area and Sacramento)

## Role

Founder and coordinator — curriculum framing, tutor coordination, and the Zoom-based delivery workflow.

## Courses

Course catalog pages were organized by level and topic filters (Arithmetic, Algebra, Geometry), including:

**High school level**

- Algebra & Linear Equations
- Functions and related intermediate topics
- Geometry
- Statistics & Probability

**College level**

- Algebra & Functions
- Trigonometry
- Statistics & Probability
- Calculus for Business
- Analytic Geometry
- Finite Math
