I develop algorithms that model multi-channel radar data as third-order tensors and use the _t_-SVD and tensor robust PCA to separate low-rank background from sparse signals of interest. The work is implemented and evaluated in MATLAB and Python.

Focus areas:

- Multi-look imaging of moving targets in clutter with stationary MIMO radar via _t_-SVD
- Clutter suppression and moving-target detection in MIMO radar
- Applications related to wide-angle and circular SAR imaging

This research connects numerical linear algebra and tensor decompositions to sensing problems in radar, with relevance to communications, navigation, and timing.
