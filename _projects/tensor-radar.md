I develop algorithms that model multi-channel radar data as third-order tensors and use the _t_-SVD and tensor robust PCA to separate low-rank background from sparse signals of interest.

Applications include:

- Detecting, separating, and imaging moving targets in clutter
- Static clutter suppression in MIMO radar
- Line-of-sight diversity for wide-angle and circular SAR

This work connects numerical linear algebra and tensor decompositions to sensing problems closely related to communications, navigation, and timing.
